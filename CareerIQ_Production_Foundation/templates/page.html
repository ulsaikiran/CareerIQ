Before you begin

Starting the assessment requests camera access and fullscreen mode. Camera processing is performed in your browser; video is not uploaded or recorded by CareerIQ. If you turn your head away for a sustained period, the system may alert faculty. Lighting, camera position and accessibility needs can affect detection, so alerts are only review signals.

Camera access requires your permission. Fullscreen is browser-controlled and may be exited; tab changes or fullscreen exits may be logged. Do not begin unless you agree to these monitoring conditions.

Camera is off until you start.

Advanced: 3 points
